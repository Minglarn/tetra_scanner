Noise Floor -44 to -35 dBm: Optimal dynamic range for RTL-SDR (typical Gain 20-28 dB).
Noise Floor > -34 dBm or Peak > -12 dBm: Front-end overload / ghost peaks. Lower Gain by 4-8 dB.

How 3-Phase Auto-Scanner Works
PHASE 1 (RF Spectrum Sweep): Measures raw RF signal power (dBm & SNR above noise floor) in 2.0 MHz blocks across the band, snapping peaks to the 25 kHz TETRA grid.
PHASE 2 (Window Selection): Evaluates all 1.8 MHz capture windows and selects up to 8 strongest candidate carriers (prioritizing verified Whitelisted cells and ignoring Blacklisted noise).
PHASE 3 (Live TETRA Decoding): Tunes GNU Radio + 8 parallel tetra-kit decoders. Frequencies broadcasting valid TETRA control frames (SYSINFO) are added to the Whitelist; silent/unmodulated RF noise carriers are Blacklisted after the 60s health check.
